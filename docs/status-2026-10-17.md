## 1. One-paragraph summary

The detector now produces reviewable candidate locations and offline image/text reports.
Three calibration studies produced eleven candidates at the existing 0.5 cutoff: eight
matched indexed references and three unmatched. The positive pair took 77.31 seconds;
the annotation-negative control took 27.92 seconds. LIDC overlaps LUNA16 training data,
so this is not unbiased accuracy. The local queue, candidate viewer and exports are
implemented. MedGemma remains experimental: multi-image binding and the new single-image
P6 categorical readiness checks failed. Earlier paired-slab scores show some image signal;
we cannot conclude that images are entirely ignored. See the
[usage review](model-usage-review-2026-10-17.md) for commands, measurements and remaining work.

## 4. Assessment of the approach

The original idea had two parts. They need different verdicts.

1. **"Benchmark local models on thoracic CT honestly"** — sound. The framework does what a
   benchmark must: it fails visibly, keeps abstentions, refuses invalid evidence and keeps
   the holdout sealed. A documented negative result is a valid result.
2. **"A small local VLM reading a few CT slices can detect findings"** — probably wrong
   as stated, at least for small nodules at study level. A 6 mm nodule is a few dozen
   pixels in one of hundreds of slices; study-level yes/no from a 4B quantized model over
   24 images is a hard, weakly supervised task, and the constant answers suggest either a
   yes-bias or that the images are not used.

Open question that must be answered before redesigning: **does the model see the images
at all?** The constant Q8 output could be a broken or ignored image path (Ollama/GGUF
vision projector, multi-image handling, chat template) rather than a capability limit.

## 5. Historical investigation plan (superseded by current plan)

In order; each step is a separately hashed, calibration-only configuration.

| # | Step | Decides | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Image-path sanity tests:** real vs blank vs noise vs shuffled images; synthetic phantom with and without an obvious nodule; free-text image description | Whether the model uses the images at all | 🔄 running |
| 2 | **Paired slab test:** one lesion-centred 5 mm slab and one matched control slab per reference nodule (≈40 pairs), one image and one yes/no question each; slice-level AUROC, yes-rate on controls | Whether there is any visual signal; size of the yes-bias; ~8× more statistical power than study level | 🔄 running |
| 3 | Rule out runtime/quantization (Hugging Face bf16 on CPU, if weights are available without new terms) | Whether Ollama Q4/Q8 is the cause | 🔄 if cheap |
| 4 | Web review of published evidence | Whether the idea is wrong | ✅ [`approach-review-2026-10-17.md`](approach-review-2026-10-17.md): **partially wrong** — benchmark sound; zero-shot study-level nodule detection by a quantized 4B VLM over 2D slices is not supported by the literature (MedGemma 1.5: 61.1 % balanced accuracy in Google's own CT set, lung-nodule F1 0.166 in an independent CT-RATE run) |
| 5 | Decide the direction (see below) and record it in `plan.md` | — | ⏳ after 1–4 |

**Likely directions, depending on steps 1–4:**

* **Image path broken:** fix the runtime path (one image per message, official interleaved
  format, different runtime) and repeat E001-schema.
* **Images used, signal at slab level but not study level:** move to a two-stage design:
  a candidate detector (classical HU/vesselness CAD, or a small MONAI detector trained
  from scratch on calibration LIDC annotations) proposes locations, and the VLM verifies
  and describes crops. Score lesion level (Task C/FROC), not only study level.
* **No signal even on obvious synthetic targets or lesion-centred slabs:** reframe: keep
  ThoraxBench as an honest benchmark that documents the failure of small local VLMs,
  add a supervised detector as the real baseline, and re-test VLMs when larger or 3D
  models (CT-CHAT, Merlin, CT-CLIP probes) become accessible.

Fine-tuning (QLoRA) stays last: it only makes sense once a visual signal exists.

### Web review findings (2026-10-17)

* Supervised 3D detectors (nnDetection, MONAI RetinaNet) reach LUNA16 CPM ≈ 0.93 with ~800
  annotated scans; generalist VLMs are near chance on small nodules.
* Each 896 px image becomes 256 tokens, so a nominal token footprint is ≈ 20 mm of chest. This is not a hard
  lesion-resolution limit. Crops (≈ 64 mm) fit the token budget far better.
* Our format differs from MedGemma's tested protocol: images likely prepended without
  markers by Ollama (to verify, test T0/T2), no reasoning before the answer, several labels
  per query, GGUF instead of bf16. Added to the running sanity tests.
* Recommended order: sanity gate → official protocol (interleaved `SLICE k`, one label per
  query, P(yes) scoring) → detector reference baseline (overlap-flagged) → VLM as crop
  verifier at lesion level → honest negative RQ1 report if gates fail. QLoRA on hold.
